# LocalShare Privacy Policy **Effective date:** November 10, 2026 ## 1. Introduction LocalShare ("the App") is an Android application that allows users to transfer files directly between devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use LocalShare. ## 2. Information and Data Handling LocalShare is designed to operate locally without requiring an account or cloud service. During normal operation, the App may process: - File names, file sizes, and file types selected for transfer. - File references and access permissions provided by Android. - Transfer status and local transfer history. - Temporary information needed to manage a transfer session. - Local network information required for device-to-device communication. This information is used to provide the App's functionality. ## 3. File Storage and Transfers Files are transferred directly between devices over Wi-Fi or a mobile hotspot. LocalShare does not intentionally upload transferred files to a developer-operated cloud server. File access is subject to Android's storage permissions and the access granted by the user. Transfer history may be stored locally on the device. ## 4. Network Security LocalShare uses a local network connection to transfer files between devices. Depending on the current implementation, the connection may use HTTP without TLS encryption. Therefore, data transmitted over the local network may not have transport-layer encryption. We recommend using a trusted Wi-Fi network or personal hotspot and avoiding public or untrusted networks when transferring sensitive files. ## 5. Data Sharing LocalShare does not sell or rent users' personal information. The App is not designed to share transferred files with advertisers or data brokers. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Transfer history and other locally stored application data may remain on the device until deleted by the user or removed through Android's application-data controls. Temporary files created during transfer are intended to be removed when no longer needed. ## 7. Children's Privacy LocalShare is not specifically directed at children. The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or cloud service. ## 8. Third-Party Services LocalShare's core file-transfer functionality is designed to operate locally and does not require a third-party cloud storage service. Third-party services provided by Android or Google Play may process information according to their own privacy policies. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. ## 10.
